Your table, your rules

Every family plays Hand and Foot a little differently. Set the meld needed in each round, the number of decks, what red threes are worth, how many wild cards a book may hold, whether the pile needs a pair, and more. Save it as a preset and every new game deals the way your table plays.

Easy to read, easy to follow

  • Large, clear cards, with your hand and both teams' melds on one screen.
  • Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion, in light or dark appearance.
  • How to play in seven short pages, and a score sheet that shows every line.
  • Your game is saved as you play, so you can always pick up where you left off.
