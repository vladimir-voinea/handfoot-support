Privacy policy

Data Not Collected

Hand and Foot does not collect any data about you or how you play.

Effective 26 October 2026

This policy covers the Hand and Foot app, published by Parallax Technologies SRL ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not run a server for the app, so nothing you do in it is sent to us.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress and the house rules presets you save are stored in the app's own storage on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional Canasta and Samba pack is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions are welcome at parallax.tech.ro@gmail.com.
