Playing

How do I learn the rules?

On Home, tap How to play. Seven short pages walk through the game, about five minutes, and they use the numbers of the rules you have set.

Why can't I change the rules during a game?

A game keeps the rules it was dealt with, so every hand plays the same way. What you save in House rules applies from your next new game.

How do I save my own house rules?

Open House rules, change any rule, and tap Save as a new preset. Give it a name, and your next new game deals with it. Standard is built in and always there.

Why does Rosa ask me before she goes out?

Rosa is your partner, so she checks with you first, just as a partner would at a real table. Answer Yes, go out or No, keep playing.

Will I lose my game if I close the app?

No. The game is saved after every move, and the app opens on the same hand next time. The saved game lives only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it too.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Hand and Foot plays fully offline. Only buying or restoring the Canasta and Samba pack goes through the App Store.

The Canasta and Samba pack

What is in the pack?

Two more partnership games, Canasta and Samba, against the same computer players. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription. To see it, tap New game, then Canasta or Samba. You can try a Canasta hand before you buy.

I bought the pack. How do I get it on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open New game, tap Canasta or Samba, and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
